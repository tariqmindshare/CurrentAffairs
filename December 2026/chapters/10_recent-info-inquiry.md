### Recent Info Inquiry

#### Bangladesh

**Ques:** What was the average pass rate in the HSC and equivalent examinations 2026?

**Ans:** 52.12% (results published on 31 October 2026).

**Ques:** How many candidates obtained GPA-5 in the HSC and equivalent examinations 2026?

**Ans:** 40,084.

**Ques:** When did Prime Minister Tarique Rahman visit Japan?

**Ans:** From 26 to 29 October 2026.

**Ques:** How many private-sector MoUs were signed through JETRO during the Prime Minister's Japan visit?

**Ans:** 17.

**Ques:** When was the six-member new minimum wage board for the garment sector formed?

**Ans:** On 1 November 2026.

**Ques:** When was the 'Special Solar Power Installation Bill, 2026' passed in Parliament?

**Ans:** On 18 November 2026.

**Ques:** What was Bangladesh's inflation rate in October 2026?

**Ans:** 8.27%.

**Ques:** Which Bangladeshi university ranked best in the QS Asia University Rankings 2027, and at what position?

**Ans:** University of Dhaka, 136th.

**Ques:** Who became the first Bangladeshi to score 7,000 runs in Test cricket?

**Ans:** Mushfiqur Rahim.

#### International

**Ques:** By what margin of judges' votes did the ICJ find Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya on 19 November 2026?

**Ans:** 9-6.

**Ques:** Which ICJ President read out the Rohingya genocide judgment?

**Ans:** Judge Yuji Iwasawa of Japan.

**Ques:** Which country, and which city, will host APEC 2027?

**Ans:** Vietnam, Phu Quoc.

**Ques:** Who became the first elected Muslim senator of the United States?

**Ans:** Abdul El-Sayed (Michigan).

**Ques:** By what vote did Greenland's parliament approve the Arctic security deal with the US and Denmark on 3 November 2026?

**Ans:** 16-0.

**Ques:** Which spacecraft became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth?

**Ans:** NASA's Voyager 1 (18 November 2026).

**Ques:** Which asteroid pair has ESA's Hera spacecraft reached?

**Ans:** Didymos and Dimorphos.

**Ques:** Who became the oldest winner in the history of the Booker Prize?

**Ans:** M. John Harrison, aged 81, for 'The End of Everything'.

**Ques:** What is the theme of World Diabetes Day 2026?

**Ans:** Early action on diabetes.

**Ques:** Which university topped the QS Asia University Rankings 2027?

**Ans:** The University of Hong Kong.

**Ques:** Which actor, famous as Captain Picard in 'Star Trek', died on 28 October 2026?

**Ans:** Sir Patrick Stewart.
